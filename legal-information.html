2. ICO & data protection fee (UK)

You do not send your website to the ICO. The Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) is the UK regulator for data protection. Most organisations that process personal data (for example: table bookings, customer contact details, staff records, CCTV, or marketing lists) must register and pay an annual data protection fee, unless a narrow exemption applies.

Use the ICO's official self-assessment to check whether you must pay:

  • ICO – Data protection fee
  • ICO – Registration

Once registered, your entry may appear on the ICO register of fee payers. Personal data processing must still comply with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018—registration is separate from having a good Privacy Policy.

4. Food allergens & menu information

We take food safety seriously. Our kitchen handles multiple allergens. While we try to accommodate dietary requirements, we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from allergens due to shared equipment and preparation areas.

Always tell our staff about any allergy or intolerance before you order. Full allergen information for our dishes is available from our team on request in the restaurant. Online menus are for guidance; ingredients and recipes may change.

For official guidance for consumers, see the Food Standards Agency – food allergy and intolerance.
